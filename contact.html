5. Account Deletion Help

The normal way to delete your account is the authenticated, self-service Delete Account control inside the app — you delete your own account while signed in. The support email is only for help or clarification; it is not the normal way to delete an account, and because accounts are anonymous, NearBuddy cannot safely identify and delete an account from an unverified email request. Information about deletion is also available at:

https://near-buddy.com/delete-account

Please remember that deletion is permanent: deleted accounts and content cannot be restored. Deleting your NearBuddy account does not by itself cancel or refund a Google Play or Apple subscription — manage renewal separately through the relevant store. See the NearBuddy Account Deletion Policy for the complete rules.

Payment and Subscription Support

Subscriptions are purchased through Google Play or the Apple App Store, depending on your device, and you manage your subscription and cancellation through the relevant store. 7. Refund and Cancellation Guidance

Refund requests and refund decisions are controlled by Google Play or Apple under their own policies and applicable law. NearBuddy does not itself issue app-store subscription refunds and does not guarantee any refund. To cancel future renewals, cancel your subscription in your Google Play or Apple account settings; cancelling stops future renewals but does not, by itself, remove access already paid for. Full details are in the NearBuddy Refund & Cancellation Policy.

10. Urgent Danger

NearBuddy is not an emergency-response service. If you or another person is in immediate danger, contact your appropriate local emergency services or law-enforcement authority right away — do not rely only on email support for an emergency. Where it is safe and lawful, keep any relevant information, but do not put yourself at additional risk to collect evidence.

11. Information to Include When Contacting Us

To help us understand your issue, where it is safe and relevant you may include:

  • a clear description of the issue;
  • the feature or page involved;
  • the approximate date and time it happened;
  • screenshots that do not expose unnecessary sensitive information;
  • the other person's visible NearBuddy profile details, where relevant;
  • for a payment question, the relevant store transaction information — without sending your card or bank credentials.

12. Information Not to Send

For your safety, please do not email us: passwords; one-time codes (OTPs); PINs; full card details; bank credentials; government-ID documents (unless specifically and lawfully required); intimate content; or any unnecessary sensitive personal information.

13. Official Communication Safety

NearBuddy's only official public contact email is namavitech@gmail.com. Be cautious of anyone claiming to represent NearBuddy from a different personal address or an unofficial account. NearBuddy will not ask you by email to send passwords, OTPs, PINs, or full payment-card or bank credentials. Subscription purchases must be completed through Google Play or the Apple App Store, as applicable, and you should use only the official NearBuddy website and official distribution links.
