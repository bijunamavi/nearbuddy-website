NearBuddy is made by NAMAVI TECHNOLOGIES PRIVATE LIMITED. This page explains the official ways to get NearBuddy and how to stay safe from fake or modified copies. Always download NearBuddy from an official source listed here.

How to get NearBuddy

There are two kinds of official route:

  • The official app stores — Google Play (for Android) and the Apple App Store (for iOS), where NearBuddy is offered for those platforms.
  • The official direct download — the NearBuddy Android app file (APK) provided from the official NearBuddy website and its official GitHub Releases.

The routes that are currently available, and the platforms NearBuddy currently supports, are shown on the official listings and on the official NearBuddy website. Please use only the official sources below.

Android and iOS via the official app stores

Where NearBuddy is offered on a store, installing from the store is the simplest and safest option:

  • Android — install NearBuddy from its official Google Play listing.
  • iOS — install NearBuddy from its official Apple App Store listing.

Installing from a store means the store handles installation, updates, and any purchases. To confirm you have the genuine app on Android, check that the listing and installed app use the official package name com.nearbuddy.mobile.

Official direct download (APK)

For Android, NearBuddy may also be offered as a direct app file (APK) from:

  • the official NearBuddy website at https://near-buddy.com, and
  • the official NearBuddy GitHub Releases linked from that website.

Only use the direct-download link provided on the official NearBuddy website. Each official release publishes its details — such as the version and a file checksum — on the official download page, so you can confirm that what you download matches the official release. The official Android package is com.nearbuddy.mobile.

Version, compatibility, price, and availability

  • Version and release details are shown on the official store listing and on the official download page for each release.
  • Device and operating-system compatibility (the versions and devices NearBuddy supports) is shown on the official listing and download page.
  • Price — NearBuddy is free to download and use. Its optional Ad-Free Subscription is purchased through Google Play or the Apple App Store, and the current price is shown by the app store before you buy. NearBuddy does not sell subscriptions through any other channel and never collects your card or bank details directly.
  • Availability — which platforms and regions NearBuddy is currently available in is shown on the official listings and website.

Because these details can change with each release, please rely on the official listing or download page for the current version, compatibility, price, and availability rather than on any figure quoted elsewhere.

Use only official sources — avoid unofficial or modified copies

Do not download NearBuddy from unofficial websites, file-sharing sites, or third parties, and do not install any "modded," "cracked," "premium-unlocked," or otherwise modified version of NearBuddy. Copies from unofficial sources can be tampered with and may contain malware, steal your information, or behave in ways NearBuddy does not control. Only the app-store listings above and the official NearBuddy website and its official GitHub Releases are genuine sources. If a link, price, or "free premium" offer looks unofficial or too good to be true, do not use it.

Installing and updating

  • From a store: install and update NearBuddy through Google Play or the Apple App Store as usual; store updates keep you on the latest version.
  • From the official APK: to install a direct APK on Android, you may need to allow installation from the source you are using in your device settings; only do this for the official NearBuddy download, and turn the setting off again afterwards if you prefer. Where a checksum is published for a release, you can verify your downloaded file against it.
  • Keep NearBuddy updated so you have the latest features, fixes, and safety improvements. If you use the direct APK, check the official website for newer releases.
