The Ad-Free Subscription is auto-renewing. Unless you cancel it, it automatically renews at the end of each subscription period, and the applicable app store charges you for the next period using your chosen payment method, at the price then shown by the app store.

If you cancel, your cancellation stops future renewals. Your paid Ad-Free access continues until the verified end of the period you have already paid for, and then ends. Access may end earlier if the app store revokes or refunds the purchase, or where applicable law requires otherwise.
